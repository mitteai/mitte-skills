Track something in a video: follow points, a flat surface (screen, sign), a masked object, the camera's shake, the camera's 3D move (camera solve), or a face, and return a track file a motion document can attach graphics to. Analysis only; it renders nothing. method=ml is reserved for a hosted tracker that is not available yet.

Use cases:

- Pin a label or graphic to a moving object in the user's footage.
- Replace a phone or laptop screen in a shot with rendered UI (planar track + pin).
- Stabilize shaky footage before designing graphics over it.
- Place 3D titles on the floor or a wall of a moving shot (camera solve + solve anchors).
- Follow a face or one of its landmarks (eyes, nose tip, mouth corners) with a label or mask.

```text
Required: video (https URL); type (point|planar|mask|stabilize|camera|face); items for point/planar/mask/face, in source pixels on the frame at from (face: {"host": {"at": [x, y]}}, a point on the face's skin).
Optional: from; range; direction (forward|backward|both); method (classic; ml is not available yet and is refused); options (channel luminance|red|green|blue|saturation|rgb, ...); corrections ([{t, item, at|corners|outline}]: fixes on bad frames; outline for mask items); base (a previous track URL); preview ("sheet": a contact sheet with the track drawn, coloured by confidence); camera ({focal: "fixed"|"variable"|horizontal angle of view in degrees, features: 400, ground: [[x, y], ...] three or more points on the floor at from}).
Camera solves always run as jobs. The camera track drives a camera layer with `solve` and places 3D nulls on the ground plane or solved points (`anchors`).
Output: the track file, the optional sheet; data per item: tracked, lost, corrected counts, mean confidence, lost spans; method; ms.
Errors: item outside the frame; search smaller than feature; too little texture (or no face at the point); under 8 features in a region; a camera that cannot be solved (no parallax or rotation); clip too long. Warning: a camera solve above 1.5 px mean reprojection error.
Notes: always use preview="sheet"; mean confidence ≥ 0.85 with no lost span over 0.5 s in the part you use means usable. Use the user's video_pick from context when present; otherwise locate the object on the first frame with vision or segmentation. Attach with a track reference, pin, or stabilize (see skill:rexx/effects/62-tracking-documents.md).
Related: motion_check; motion_frames.
```
