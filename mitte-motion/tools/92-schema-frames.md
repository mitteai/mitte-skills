Render chosen moments of a motion document as still images: single frames at given times or at the piece's key moments, or one contact-sheet grid with timestamps. Fast; for checking work before the final render.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `category` | `motion` |
| `cost` | 2 |
| `timeout_ms` | 120000 (2 min) |
| `sandbox` | `local` |
| `sync` | true |
| `resource_limits` | 4 CPU / 2048 MiB / 256 pids |
| `output_asset_type` | `image` |

Use cases:

- See a motion graphics piece (a motion film, title sequence, explainer or social ad) as stills before paying for the render.
- Review a piece's layout and timing from a contact sheet with trails before rendering.
- Check held text against a platform's safe zone at specific moments.
- Compare physics takes across seeds on one sheet before locking one.

Instructions (verbatim, what the assistant reads):

```text
Required: doc or doc_url (exactly one); and one of times (up to 24: seconds, "12f", "@beat4", "50%", "end-0.5") or auto (N frames at the key moments).
Optional: variant; data; sheet (true: one contact-sheet PNG, up to 6 x 4 cells); width (px, default 960); safe_overlay (tiktok|reels|shorts|youtube|…); transparent; motion_blur (default true); trails (true: ghosts of moving layers at neighbouring frames, so motion shows in a still); seeds (up to 6, with sheet=true: one row of physics takes per seed).
Output: one PNG per time or one sheet; data with times, cell indices, width, height, diagnostics, ms.
Errors: the document has errors (run motion_check first); both or neither of times and auto; over 24 frames; a time outside the comp.
Notes: auto with sheet=true and trails=true reviews a piece; single times with safe_overlay check held text. Judge the images against the checklist before rendering.
Related: motion_check (first); motion_render.
```
