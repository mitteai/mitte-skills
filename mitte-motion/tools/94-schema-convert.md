Convert between motion documents and other motion formats: export to Lottie/dotLottie (web and app playback), an After Effects script (.jsx) that rebuilds the composition in After Effects, or the comp's camera path for a matching 3D clip; import Lottie, dotLottie or SVG into a motion document.

Use cases:

- Export a finished document to Lottie or dotLottie for web and app playback.
- Hand a composition to a human designer as an After Effects script with its assets.
- Import an existing Lottie animation or an SVG as an editable document to restyle.
- Export the camera path of a 3D comp so mesh_scene_video films a real 3D element (an extruded title, a model) through the same camera, then lay the clip back into the document.

Instructions (verbatim, what the assistant reads):

```text
Required: for export, doc or doc_url and to (lottie|dotlottie|aejsx|canonical|camera); for import, from (lottie, dotlottie or svg) and url.
Optional: variant; data; options (Lottie and dotLottie: text layer|outlines, bake_expressions, bake_fps, bake_tolerance, effects drop|players|render, embed_images, render_unsupported; dotLottie: themes; After Effects: bake_fps, bake_tolerance, bake_expressions, ae_min_version, font_map, ae_prerender; import: comp_id; svg: fps, duration, layers; lottie and dotlottie: theme, animation).
Particle layers have no Lottie or After Effects form. Lottie and dotLottie drop them unless render_unsupported is true, which embeds each as a rendered image sequence (a larger file); effects: render does the same for every layer that has effects, and like ae_prerender it is skipped (effects dropped, convert.prerender_skipped) when the engine's estimate for the renders is above this call's prerender budget. After Effects renders each to a ProRes 4444 clip with alpha by default (ae_prerender; false drops them), placed in a precomp the script builds; with ae_prerender unset, the clips are skipped when the engine's estimate for them is above this call's prerender budget, a quarter of its 120 s timeout (the warning convert.prerender_skipped and data.prerender_skipped say so with the estimate; pass ae_prerender true to render them anyway, which may time out).
Output: the converted file (lottie: main.json; dotlottie: main.lottie, a zip; aejsx: main.jsx; canonical: main.motion.json; import: <source>.motion.json; camera: main.camera.json, the root comp's active camera at every frame in comp pixels: size, fps_rational, frame_count, and per frame position, quaternion, euler_xyz_deg, a camera-to-world matrix, zoom, film_size_mm, focal_length_mm, fov_deg, dof; its convention object states the axes and projection), plus one image file per referenced image when embed_images is false (stored as images/<file> next to the JSON, so its relative references resolve) and, for After Effects with ae_prerender, one video file per rendered particle layer (stored as assets/<file>.mov next to the script, which imports it from there); data.warnings (code, path, message) for every feature the target cannot represent and what was done instead; data.coverage (share of layers exported with no loss); data.prerender_skipped (estimate_seconds, budget_seconds, layers) when an After Effects export left its particle clips out.
Errors: the document has errors; an option with a value outside its list (doc.enum at /options/<name>); the source is not valid SVG, not a Lottie animation, or above the Lottie caps (limit.lottie); a target this build cannot convert (convert.unavailable).
Notes: The camera path is the 3D-clip hand-off (spec 16 §16.9): pass its URL as camera_path_url to mesh_scene_video (seconds at least the comp's duration; pixels_per_meter 100 puts the comp's center on the z = 0 plane at the scene's origin, y up), and put the returned ProRes 4444 clip in the document as a plain 2D video layer filling the comp (not threeD: it is already filmed through the camera), between the 3D layers it should sit in front of and the text, glow and grade above it. Lottie cannot carry most effects, blend modes, video, cameras, or advanced text animators — read warnings. The After Effects export is the hand-off for a human designer. An imported SVG is an editable document you can restyle. An imported Lottie keeps its layer names as ids; expressions outside the expression subset are dropped (convert.lottie_expression) with their keyframes kept, fonts outside the font pack become Inter (convert.font_substituted), and the images, fonts and audio it embeds come back as extra files (images/, fonts/, audio/<file> next to the document) that its assets already point at. To play a Lottie inside a composition instead, make it a comp layer's lottie source.
Related: motion_check; motion_render; mesh_scene_video (films a 3D scene through the camera path).
```
