### `mitte/film-burn@1`

A procedural film burn: hot, saturated orange and red blooms eat in from one edge of the frame, flare to a near-white flash at the peak, and recede. Additive, so it blows out what is under it like burnt celluloid. Full frame, any aspect ratio, no assets. Cut between two shots under its peak (at 45 % of `duration`) for a film-burn transition.

Notes from the library file:

- `lint.motion_blur_off_fast_motion` is waived: the blobs are soft gradients with no edge to strobe, and motion blur would only add render cost. Radial-gradient ellipses with `add` blend and a hot solid for the peak flash, instead of the `stylize.ccBurnFilm` effect, so the burn exports to Lottie as shapes (the `add` blend maps to Lottie's `add`). Positions are percentages of the parent and blob sizes multiples of `$theme.space.9`, so the burn starts at the chosen edge in every aspect ratio. The `filmBurn` transition type still draws a flat solid; wiring it to this component is an engine change.

### `mitte/light-leak@1`

A procedural light leak: warm, soft blobs of light hug one edge of the frame, bloom in, drift inwards and swell, then fade. Screen-blended, so it only ever brightens what is under it; place it above footage or scenes. Full frame, any aspect ratio, no assets. Used on its own as a warm accent, or by `transition.light-leak` to hide a cut.

Notes from the library file:

- Built from radial-gradient ellipses with `screen` blend instead of the `light.leak` effect, so it exports to Lottie as plain shapes (gradient fills and blend modes both map). Blob sizes are multiples of `$theme.space.9` (0.3 × the parent's shorter side) because shape item sizes take no percentages; positions are percentages of the parent so the leak hugs the chosen edge in every aspect ratio. The spec's `lightLeak` transition type still draws a flat screen-blended solid; wiring it to this component is an engine change (see the P3 library report).
