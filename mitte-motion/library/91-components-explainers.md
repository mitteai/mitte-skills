### `mitte/explainer.step@1`

Explainer step: a numbered (or symbol) badge pops in inside a ring that draws on, the title and one or two sentences of body text rise in, and a dotted connector draws down towards the next step. After `hold` the text fades and the badge scales out. Sequence several instances on voice-over markers (`in: "@step2"`) for faceless explainers and how-to videos.

| `connector` | boolean | `true` | — | Draw a line down from the badge towards the next step once the step has landed. Turn it off on the last step. |
| `hold` | time | `4` | 1–60 | Seconds the step stays fully visible before it leaves. Match it to the voice-over between this step's marker and the next. |

Notes from the library file:

- The number and the symbol are two text layers switched by `enabled` on `icon`; the connector is switched by `connector`, so the last step of a sequence turns it off. Title and body animate as whole layers (not per word) so a Lottie export keeps their motion. The canvas is 1000 × 400 with the badge at its left; place instances with `origin: "center"` or align them to the safe area's left edge.
