Notes from the library file:

- The bar and label box are `fitTo` rectangles with minimum sizes, so short headlines still get a broadcast-width bar (1200 px). The headline is box text 86 % of the frame wide less about 340 px for the label box, two lines tall, with `overflow: shrink`: a headline too long for one line wraps onto a second (in 16:9 only past about 50 characters) and shrinks only when two lines cannot hold it, so in 9:16 it stays inside the frame instead of being cut mid-word. The bar keeps its 1200 px minimum, so in 9:16 the empty end of the bar still runs off the right edge (the strap convention); the words do not.
