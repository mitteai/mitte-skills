### `mitte/lyrics@1`

Lyric lines timed to the music: each line rises in just before it is sung, fills from a dim colour to full colour character by character across its sung span (a karaoke sweep driven by the line's start and end), punches up slightly on its downbeat, and lifts away when it ends. Feed it line timings from a transcription or lyric-sync tool. One line on screen at a time, centred, wrapping long lines (up to three) and shrinking only what still does not fit.

Notes from the library file:

- Per-line timing lives in each generated layer's own keys (a `repeat` template cannot carry timeline tweens), with times templated from `$item.start` and `$item.end`. The sweep is a `range` text animator on `fill` whose `end` is keyed from 0 to 100 % across the sung span; Lottie exports it as a lottie-web range selector. Lines hand over without overlapping: a line lifts away over 0.45 s after it ends, or by the time the next line would fade in (`start − lead`) when that is sooner (never less than 0.1 s), and a line fades in no earlier than the previous one has gone. The neighbours' times come from `sum($props.lines.start, $index + 1, $index + 2)` and `sum($props.lines.end, $index - 1, $index)`, which are 0 past either end of the list (the comparisons with `$index` and `$count` switch them off there). `lint.text_too_short_on_screen` is expected for fast lines and is not a defect of the component: lyric lines are on screen exactly as long as they are sung.
