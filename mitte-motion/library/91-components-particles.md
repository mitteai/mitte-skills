Notes from the library file:

- The unlit cloudlets render at `resolution: 50` (spec 9 §9.8: very soft particles upscale invisibly): 180 cloudlets hundreds of pixels wide cost about a quarter of the full-resolution drawing time. With a `light` the 3D `litSmoke` layer draws instead (shadowlets need the 3D path; the instance's light is copied into the component as `_host.<id>`, spec 12 §12.3.2) at full resolution: `render.resolution` on a 3D particle layer shrinks the particles instead of only the buffer (an engine bug, reported).
