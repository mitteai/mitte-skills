What the library file says the normalizer derives (see `05-captions.md` and `06-credits.md` for what engine 0.1.48 actually builds):

- `sheet`: every section is typeset into its own layers (measured, `width` wide, centred), stacked top to bottom with `lineHeight × size` leading and `2 × lineHeight × size` between sections, all parented to `sheet`, an unfilled rect exactly the block's size; the `crawl` (whole-pixel speed, `motionBlur: false`, `hold` [1, 1] = one second of black head and tail) moves `sheet`
- `card`: `tplRole` then `tplNames`; several names on one line joined with ` & ` or ` and ` per `join`; names larger or heavier than roles (`nameWeight` > `roleWeight`; role size = round(0.85 × size))
- `heading`: `tplHeading` with an extra half line of space above
- `pairs`: two columns on a centre gutter: `tplPairRole` right-aligned ending at centre − gutter/2, `tplPairNames` left-aligned starting at centre + gutter/2, one row per entry; extra names stack under the first
- `list`: title as `tplRole`, then one `tplCell` per name, centred
- `grid`: title as `tplRole`, then `tplCell` in `columns` columns (section `columns` 0 → the component's `columns`; 0 there too → auto 3–6 by longest name) with `gutter` between columns, row-major
- `song`: `tplSongTitle` then one `tplLine` per line
- `text`: `tplLegal` style at `size`
- `legal`: `tplLegal` at round(0.75 × size)
- `logos`: `tplLogos` with `count` = number of `image_urls`, assets `<instance>.img<n>`, logos fitted into 320 × 160 at 1080p (scaled with `size` / 28)
- `photo`: skipped with `lint.credits_kind_skipped` (spec 12 §12.8: a photo needs a card; `credits.cards` draws it)
- `namesCase`: `upper` upper-cases names but keeps the letter after `Mc`, `Mac`, `O'`, `Di`, `De`, `Van`, `Von` prefixes lowercase; `title` title-cases; `none` leaves them
- `speed`: with `duration`, speed = round(L / (duration × fps)) clamped ≥ 1, actual = L / speed frames, reported in the timing sheet; `lint.crawl_duration_mismatch` above 5 %; `lint.crawl_too_fast` above 4 px/frame at 24 fps (scaled by fps / 24)
- `weights`: `nameWeight` and `roleWeight` are clamped to 400–700; `lineHeight` to 1.2–1.45; `white` lighter than #EBEBEB is clamped to it with `lint.credits_white_hot`

Notes from the library file:

- Engine-backed: typesetting a heterogeneous `sections` list (kind-dependent layouts, running heights, auto columns, `join`, name casing) has no document-grammar form, so the normalizer (`passes/credits.rs`) builds the layers and applies the `crawl` to the `sheet` carrier. The `tpl*` layers below are reference looks, not stamped. `x-engine.derives` names every rule; `photo` sections are skipped here with `lint.credits_kind_skipped` (they need a card).
